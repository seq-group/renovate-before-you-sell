01 / THE APPROACHLESS, BUT BETTER

Not every improvement
is an investment.

Before the paint samples and the trade quotes, take a step back. The right preparation makes a home easier to understand, easier to love and easier to move into. It doesn’t have to mean a renovation.

01 · SPEND

Make the everyday
look its best.

Start with cleaning, decluttering, lighting and the garden. Small, visible changes can lift the way a home presents.

Clear surfaces. Clean glass. A welcoming entry.
02 · FIX

Put the important
things right.

Investigate leaks, damage and safety concerns. Use qualified trades and keep records of the work.

Sound repairs before fresh paint.
03 · ASK FIRST

Price the idea.
Then question it.

Flooring, a kitchen refresh or exterior paint may make sense. Check the condition, the timing and your likely buyers.

Get a second opinion before a big quote.
04 · SKIP

Leave something
for the next owner.

A new pool, extension or full renovation is usually more than a pre-sale refresh needs. Buyers may have their own plans.

Know when to put the tools down.
02 / THE BUYER’S EYE

Let the room
do the talking.

What does a buyer see from the doorway? Start there.

  1. Light, before lighting.Clean the windows. Open the blinds. Replace blown globes.
  2. Space, before more space.Clear the benchtops and pack away what you won’t need.
  3. Care, before a complete makeover.Fix the sticking door. Clean the grout. Tidy the entry.

05 / THE IMPORTANT DETAILSQUEENSLAND SELLERS

Beautiful is good.
Prepared is better.

Get these conversations started early. Your solicitor, conveyancer and licensed inspectors can confirm what applies to your property.

01 Smoke alarms +

Homes sold in Queensland need compliant interconnected photoelectric smoke alarms, including in bedrooms, connecting hallways and on each storey. From 1 January 2027 the requirements extend to all existing private homes. Ask a qualified installer about your property.

Queensland Fire Department ↗
02 Pool safety +

If you sell without a valid pool safety certificate, prescribed notice requirements apply. For a non-shared pool, the buyer generally has 90 days from settlement to obtain a certificate. Shared pools have different responsibilities. Confirm the process with your solicitor and pool inspector.

QBCC pool guidance ↗
03 Seller disclosure +

Queensland’s seller disclosure scheme commenced on 1 August 2025. Unless an exemption applies, the seller disclosure statement (Form 2) and prescribed certificates must be provided before the buyer signs. Engage your solicitor early to prepare the right documents.

Queensland Government guidance ↗

General information, reviewed 26 October 2026. Confirm current requirements for your property with the relevant professional.

07 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
begin.

Should I replace the kitchen? +

Start with its condition and what buyers at your price point expect. Cleaning or a targeted refresh may be enough. Compare the cost and timing of a full replacement with a simpler option before committing.

Is the walkthrough really free? +
